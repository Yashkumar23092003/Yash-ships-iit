

# Yash Kumar — Portfolio Website

## Design System
- **Background**: #0A0A0A base, #1A1A1A cards, #F0FF44 electric lime accent
- **Fonts**: Syne (headings) + DM Mono (body/accents) via Google Fonts
- **Noise texture overlay** on background for depth
- **Custom cursor**: lime dot with slight trail
- **Framer Motion** scroll animations throughout (fade-up, stagger)

## Sections (top to bottom)

### 1. Hero (fullscreen)
- Word-by-word animated headline: "I turn messy ideas into shipping products."
- Subtitle: "IIT Delhi Alumnus · AI Product Manager · Startup Founder"
- Two CTAs: [View My Work ↓] smooth-scrolls down, [Book a 1:1 →] links to cal.com
- "Open to PM Roles" floating badge top-right
- Subtle floating lime geometric shapes (low opacity) in background

### 2. Stats Bar
- 5-column grid (scrollable on mobile) with stats: 10,000+ Users, 150+ Interviews, 3 Products, AIR 1380, Top 25 Hackathon
- Monospaced font, lime numbers

### 3. About / The Story
- Two-column: punchy bio left, terminal-style info card right
- Terminal card shows name, role, location, current company, status

### 4. Experience Timeline
- Vertical alternating timeline (desktop), single column (mobile)
- 4 experience cards with tags, dates, descriptions as specified

### 5. Skills — "The Toolkit"
- Two columns: "Product Brain" and "Engineering Hands"
- Lime pill/tag style for each skill

### 6. Achievements — "Recognition"
- 3-column card grid with icon, stat, label for 6 achievements

### 7. Book a 1:1
- Bold CTA section, lime button linking to cal.com
- Supporting text beneath

### 8. Footer
- Minimal: Name, LinkedIn, Email, Phone
- Tagline: "Built by Yash..."

## Technical
- Framer Motion for all scroll animations (fade-up, stagger)
- Smooth scroll behavior, mobile-first responsive
- All external links open in new tab
- No placeholder images — geometric/abstract visuals only
